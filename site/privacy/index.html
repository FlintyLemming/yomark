有码 Yomark 隐私权政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 15 日

English version below

本政策适用于 Android 应用「有码」（英文名 Yomark，包名 moe.flinty.yomark，以下简称「本应用」），说明本应用如何处理你的数据。

一句话概括：开发者不收集、不分享任何个人数据或设备数据。图片的识别、编辑和导出全部在你的手机上完成，开发者无法看到你处理的任何内容。只有你在「实验室」里打开「外部模型」时，「AI 增强识别」才会把识别出的文字发到你自己填写的接口（见第 2 节）。

1. 本应用不收集的数据

本应用没有账号系统，没有服务器，不包含广告、统计分析或崩溃上报 SDK。我们不收集以下任何信息：

  • 你选择或分享给本应用的图片，以及从中识别出的文字、人脸、二维码等内容；
  • 姓名、电话、邮箱、地址等个人信息；
  • 设备标识符、广告 ID、位置信息；
  • 使用记录、操作日志或崩溃日志。

2. 网络访问

本应用声明了 INTERNET 权限，只用于「外部模型」。这是「实验室」里的功能，出厂关闭。你打开它并在「设置 → AI」里填写接口地址、API Key 和模型后：

  • 每次你在编辑器里点「AI 增强识别」，本应用会把这张图里识别出的文字发送到你填写的接口地址。图片本身不会发送；规则已经认出的号码（电话、证件号等）会先替换成「〔电话〕」这样的占位符；
  • 点「测试连接」时，会发送一句固定的测试文字；
  • 接口由你选择，开发者不经手、也看不到这些内容。该服务如何处理你发送的文字，适用它自己的政策。

除此之外，本应用不发出任何网络请求。本应用使用的 Google ML Kit 离线组件中带有向 Google 回传使用统计的代码，它的上报通道在构建时被移除，不会发送任何数据。

以下情况会由其他应用代为联网，均需你主动操作：

  • 应用内购买：购买「去除水印」时，支付流程在 Google Play 商店应用中完成（见第 5 节）；
  • 打开网页：点击本政策或打赏等链接时，会交给你手机上的浏览器打开，之后的访问适用该网站自己的政策。

3. 图片如何处理

  • 读取：通过系统照片选择器或「分享」选择图片，本应用只能读到你选中的那几张图，无法访问相册里的其他内容。
  • 识别：文字识别（PP-OCR、Google ML Kit）、人脸和条码识别（Google ML Kit）都使用随应用安装的离线模型，在本机运行。「AI 增强识别」默认使用手机系统自带的 Gemini Nano（通过 Android AICore），同样在本机运行；只有打开了「外部模型」，才改为把识别出的文字发到你填写的接口（见第 2 节）。本应用不会把图片发送到任何服务器。
  • 临时副本：编辑期间，图片会复制一份到本应用的私有缓存目录，其他应用无法访问，也不会离开你的手机。打开下一张图片时，上一次的副本会被删除；清除本应用的缓存或卸载本应用也会将其删除。
  • 导出：导出的图片保存在手机相册的「Pictures/Yomark」文件夹中，按原图重新绘制，不保留位置、机型、拍摄时间等 EXIF 信息。如果是其他应用请你选图（例如在聊天应用的附件菜单里选择本应用），打好码的图片会直接交给该应用，不保存到相册；交出的这一份暂存在本应用的私有缓存目录中，只授权该应用读取，下次有应用请你选图时删除。

4. 保存在本机的数据

本应用只在手机上保存以下内容，均不包含图片或识别结果：

  • 你的设置，例如各类信息的处理方式、打码样式、主题色、用途水印的外观；使用外部模型时，还有你填写的接口地址、API Key 和模型名称；
  • 是否已解锁「去除水印」。

这些数据不会被上传，也不参与系统云备份。卸载本应用后会被系统一并删除；你也可以在系统设置中清除本应用的数据。

5. 应用内购买

在 Google Play 发布的版本提供一项一次性购买：去除导出图片上的品牌水印。购买由 Google Play 处理，你的付款信息只提供给 Google，开发者无法获得。本应用只会从 Google Play 得知你是否已购买该商品，用于决定是否显示水印。Google 如何处理相关数据，请参阅 Google 隐私权政策。

6. 权限

本应用不申请任何需要你授权的运行时权限（相机、存储、位置、通讯录等）。安装包中仅包含以下不涉及读取个人数据的声明：

  • android.permission.INTERNET：用于「外部模型」（见第 2 节）；
  • com.android.vending.BILLING：用于 Google Play 应用内购买；
  • com.google.android.apps.aicore.service.BIND_SERVICE：用于调用系统的本机 AI 服务（AI 增强识别）；
  • moe.flinty.yomark.DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION：Android 组件库自动生成的内部权限，其他应用无法获取。

7. 数据分享

由于开发者不收集任何数据，我们不会向任何第三方（包括广告商、数据分析服务或其他公司）出售、分享或转让你的数据。使用「外部模型」时，文字由你的手机直接发到你自己选择的服务，不经过开发者。

8. 儿童隐私

本应用不以儿童为目标受众，也不会收集任何人的个人信息，包括儿童。

9. 开源

本应用的源代码公开在 GitHub，你可以自行查看上述说明是否属实。

10. 政策变更

如本政策有更新，我们会在此页面发布新版本并修改顶部的生效日期。

11. 联系我们

如对本政策有任何疑问，请在 GitHub Issues 中提出。

Yomark Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 15, 2026

This policy applies to the Android app Yomark (有码, package name moe.flinty.yomark, "the app") and explains how the app handles your data.

In short: the developer does not collect or share any personal or device data. Recognition, editing and export all happen on your phone. The developer cannot see anything you process. Only if you turn on "External model" in Labs does "AI review" send the recognized text to an API you set up yourself (see section 2).

1. Data we do not collect

The app has no accounts, no servers, and no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs. We do not collect:

  • images you pick or share to the app, or the text, faces, QR codes and other content recognized in them;
  • personal information such as names, phone numbers, email addresses or addresses;
  • device identifiers, advertising IDs or location;
  • usage history, activity logs or crash logs.

2. Network access

The app declares the INTERNET permission for one purpose: "External model", a Labs feature that is off by default. Once you turn it on and enter an API URL, key and model under Settings → AI:

  • each time you tap "AI review" in the editor, the app sends the text recognized in that image to the URL you entered. The image itself is never sent, and numbers the rules already found (phone numbers, ID numbers and so on) are replaced with placeholders such as "〔电话〕" first;
  • "Test connection" sends one fixed test sentence;
  • you choose the service. The developer never handles or sees this text, and that service's own policy applies to what you send it.

Apart from this, the app makes no network requests. The offline Google ML Kit components bundled in the app contain code that reports usage statistics to Google; its reporting backend is removed at build time, so it sends nothing.

Other apps go online on the app's behalf only when you act:

  • In-app purchase: buying "Remove watermark" is handled inside the Google Play Store app (see section 5);
  • Opening web pages: links such as this policy are opened in your browser, and that website's own policy applies from then on.

3. How images are handled

  • Access: images are chosen through the system photo picker or the Share menu. The app can only read the images you select and cannot access the rest of your photo library.
  • Recognition: text recognition (PP-OCR, Google ML Kit) and face and barcode detection (Google ML Kit) use offline models shipped with the app and run on your device. "AI review" uses the Gemini Nano model built into your phone's system (via Android AICore) by default, also on-device. Only with "External model" turned on does it send the recognized text to the API you entered instead (see section 2). The app never sends images to any server.
  • Temporary copies: while you edit, a copy of the image is kept in the app's private cache, which other apps cannot access, and it never leaves your phone. The previous copy is deleted when you open the next image, and clearing the app's cache or uninstalling the app also removes it.
  • Export: exported images are saved to the "Pictures/Yomark" folder on your phone. They are redrawn from scratch and carry no EXIF data such as location, device model or capture time. When another app asks you to pick an image (for example, when you choose this app from a chat app's attachment menu), the redacted image is handed to that app instead of being saved to your gallery. That copy stays in the app's private cache, readable only by the app it was handed to, and is deleted the next time an app asks you to pick an image.

4. Data stored on your device

The app stores only the following on your phone, none of which includes images or recognition results:

  • your settings, such as how each type of information is handled, mask styles, theme color and purpose-watermark appearance, plus the API URL, key and model name if you use an external model;
  • whether "Remove watermark" has been unlocked.

This data is never uploaded and is excluded from system cloud backup. It is deleted when you uninstall the app, and you can clear it at any time from the system settings.

5. In-app purchase

The version distributed on Google Play offers a single one-time purchase that removes the brand watermark from exported images. Purchases are processed by Google Play. Your payment information is provided only to Google, and the developer never receives it. The app only learns from Google Play whether you own this item, to decide whether to show the watermark. See the Google Privacy Policy for how Google handles this data.

6. Permissions

The app requests no runtime permissions (camera, storage, location, contacts and so on). The package contains only these declarations, none of which reads personal data:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: "External model" (see section 2);
  • com.android.vending.BILLING: Google Play in-app purchases;
  • com.google.android.apps.aicore.service.BIND_SERVICE: the system's on-device AI service (AI review);
  • moe.flinty.yomark.DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION: an internal permission generated by AndroidX that other apps cannot obtain.

7. Data sharing

Because the developer collects no data, we do not sell, share or transfer your data to any third party, including advertisers, analytics providers or other companies. With "External model", text goes straight from your phone to the service you chose and never passes through the developer.

8. Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

9. Open source

The app's source code is public on GitHub, so you can verify the statements above yourself.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will publish the new version on this page and update the effective date at the top.
